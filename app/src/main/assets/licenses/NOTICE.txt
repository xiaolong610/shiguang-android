The original library is built as a separate shared libmobi.so. Its complete source
and the JNI bridge/build configuration are included in the Shiguang 0.6.0 source
archive under third_party/libmobi and app/src/main/cpp. It may be modified,
recompiled and relinked by rebuilding the supplied Android project. Encryption
support is disabled; Shiguang rejects DRM-protected books.
